// FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is VAPT and why does my business need it?+

VAPT (Vulnerability Assessment and Penetration Testing) combines automated vulnerability scanning with manual, exploit-driven penetration testing. It identifies real, exploitable weaknesses in your applications, APIs, networks and cloud before attackers do, and is required or recommended by frameworks such as ISO 27001, SOC 2, PCI DSS and RBI/SEBI guidelines.

How long does a penetration test take?+

A typical web application or API penetration test takes 1–3 weeks depending on scope, followed by a remediation window and free retesting of fixed findings. Enterprise and multi-asset engagements are scoped individually during discovery.

Does CipherTrivia follow OWASP standards?+

Yes. Our methodology is aligned to OWASP Top 10, OWASP API Security Top 10, OWASP MASVS for mobile, plus PTES and NIST SP 800-115 — with manual exploitation and business-logic testing layered on top of automated scanning.

Can you help with ISO 27001, SOC 2 or PCI DSS compliance?+

Yes. We provide gap assessments, security control guidance, evidence-ready VAPT reports, and ongoing testing support for ISO 27001, SOC 2, PCI DSS, HIPAA and GDPR programs.

Who is behind CipherTrivia?+

CipherTrivia is the dedicated cybersecurity services brand powered by Nextwebi IT Solutions Pvt. Ltd., an ISO 9001 and ISO 27001 certified software engineering company based in Bengaluru, India — serving 600+ clients across 20+ countries for over a decade.
